Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PicSlide Adventure ("the App") handles information when you use our application. This policy applies to all users of the App, including users participating in closed testing on Google Play.

1. Information We Collect

The App itself does not directly collect any personal information. However, the App uses third-party services that may collect information:

  • Google AdMob — used to display advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers (Advertising ID), IP address, and usage data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services — used for ad delivery and app functionality. May collect device identifiers and usage statistics. See Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Location data (GPS, coarse, or fine location)
  • Contacts or media files
  • Microphone, camera, or storage access
  • Game progress, coins, or settings (stored locally on device only via localStorage)
  • Device identifiers for purposes other than ad personalization by AdMob

3. How We Use Data

The data collected by third-party services (AdMob and Google Play Services) is used solely for:

  • Serving advertisements within the App
  • Measuring ad performance and analytics
  • Providing a free-to-play experience supported by ads

We do not sell, share, or transfer any user data to third parties except as described in this policy.

4. Advertising

The App displays ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use advertising IDs and other information to provide relevant advertisements. You can opt out of personalized advertising by adjusting your device settings:

  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → Disable App Tracking

For more information, visit Google Ads Help.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is generally suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content follows AdMob age-appropriate settings. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

6. Data Security

All game data (progress, coins, settings) is stored locally on your device using localStorage and is never transmitted to our servers. We do not operate any servers that store user data. Data is encrypted at rest on the device.

7. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services — Privacy Policy
  • Google Terms of Service

8. Closed Testing Disclosure

This App is distributed through Google Play's closed testing program. During closed testing, your device identifier and usage data may be collected by Google and third-party ad partners in accordance with their respective privacy policies. Google Play's closed testing track does not collect any additional personal data beyond what is described in this policy.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
